JTSN · 잡툴사니 does not bundle the following third-party binaries in this package.
They are downloaded from their official distribution locations on first use and cached locally.

SumatraPDF 3.6.1
Official site: https://www.sumatrapdfreader.org/
Used for PDF printing with print settings.

qpdf 12.4.0
Official repository: https://github.com/qpdf/qpdf
Used for PDF merge / page extraction / page split operations.

PDFium preview engine
- PDFium: open-source PDF rendering engine (BSD-style license)
- Binary distribution helper: bblanchon/pdfium-binaries (MIT)
- Runtime package used by v3.7: Windows x64 PDFium binary distribution
